about.exe

Hey! I'm a Electrical & Computer System Engineering student at Monash University, currently splitting my time between two obsessions: teaching machines to see, and building tiny computers from scratch.

On the software side, I train and ship on-device computer vision models — wood classifiers, object detectors, YOLO pipelines — squeezed down to run fully offline with TFLite and ExecuTorch on real phones and cameras.

On the hardware side, I design digital systems in Verilog: a 16-bit CPU on an FPGA, an AES-128 core taped out on real SKY130 silicon through Tiny Tapeout, and embedded rigs pairing STM32/NIOS II boards with sensors and cameras.
